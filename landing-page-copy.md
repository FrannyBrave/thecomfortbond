## DESIGN NOTES (FOR THE BUILDER)

**Palette:**
- Background: warm cream (#FAF6EF) and a slightly deeper warm bronze/cream for alternating sections
- Text: dark charcoal/ink for high readability
- Accent: Terracotta (#C86552) for primary CTA buttons and warm highlights
- Secondary accent: Deep Teal (#1A4D46) or Forest Green for secondary buttons, borders, and quiet emphasis

**Typography:**
- Headlines: Cormorant Garamond (or similar elegant serif, often italic for emotional lines to match our 1960s editorial feel)
- Body: Inter or similar clean sans-serif (clean, light, generous line height)

**Imagery rules:**
- Real adult-child + parent imagery — natural light, candid, never staged
- Soft props: linen, cream paper, teacups, hands, fresh flowers
- Avoid: stock photos of "happy seniors" with thumbs up, corporate medical imagery, anything clinical

**Layout rules:**
- Generous whitespace between sections (90–120px vertical padding)
- Single column for emotional sections (max 680px wide)
- Two-column only for the free offer + product cards
- No icons, no badges, no "as seen in" logos — keep it personal, not institutional
